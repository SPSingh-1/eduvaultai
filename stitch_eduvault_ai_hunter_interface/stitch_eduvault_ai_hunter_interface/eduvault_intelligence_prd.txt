# EduVault AI Sales Hunter - Final Intelligence Layer PRD

## Core Vision
To provide a complete 'Executive Control Tower' for the entire revenue operation, connecting market discovery, competitive intelligence, and team performance into a single autonomous loop.

## Key Modules
1. **Market & Search Intelligence**: Discovering demand before it becomes obvious.
2. **Competitive Radar**: Monitoring the market landscape via public signals.
3. **Sales Playbook & Training**: Transforming AI insights into repeatable human performance.
4. **AI Simulation Studio**: Immersive practice for sales reps against AI-driven school principals.
5. **Executive Command Center**: 30-second business overview for leadership.
6. **AI Control Tower**: Real-time visualization of the entire autonomous workforce.

## Design Principles
- **Explainability**: Every AI recommendation shows the 'Why'.
- **Autonomy with Control**: Humans set the policy, AI executes the mission.
- **High Density**: Professional-grade workspaces for serious enterprise growth.
- **Real-time**: The platform breathes with the market.
